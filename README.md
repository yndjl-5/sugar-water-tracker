# Sugar & Water Tracker 💧🍬

## About the App

Sugar & Water Tracker is an app designed to help people keep track of how much sugar and water they consume every day.

The idea for this app came from noticing how people are becoming more aware of their diet and health. At the same time, drinks like bubble tea, coffee, soda, and other sweet drinks have become a part of many people's daily lives. Some people may drink one or even more sugary drinks every day, but forget to drink enough water.

Because of this, it can be difficult to know how much sugar we are actually consuming and whether we are drinking enough water.

This app makes it easier for users to record both in one place.

## Main Features

- 💧 Record daily water intake
- 🍬 Record daily sugar intake
- 🧋 Add drinks such as bubble tea, coffee, or soda
- 📊 See daily sugar and water progress
- 🎯 Set personal daily goals
- 📅 View previous records
- 🔔 Remind users to drink water

## Goal

The goal of this app is not to tell users that they cannot drink sugary drinks. Instead, it helps users become more aware of their daily habits.

For example, if someone drinks a bubble tea, they can record the amount of sugar in the app. They can also see how much water they have consumed that day.

By showing both sugar and water intake together, the app can encourage users to make more balanced choices and develop healthier daily habits.

## Target Users

This app is designed for anyone who wants to pay more attention to their daily diet, especially:

- Students
- People who often drink bubble tea or other sweet drinks
- People who forget to drink water
- People who want to control their sugar intake

## Future Ideas

In the future, the app could include:

- Sugar information for popular drinks
- Different sugar levels (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)
- Personalized water goals
- Weekly and monthly statistics
- Notifications when sugar intake is high
- Achievements for reaching water goals
